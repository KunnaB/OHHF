Lamped was born from a desire to improve personal Bible study and make it easy to archive — always at the tip of your fingers, always within reach.

It offers targeted, focused Bible study with minimal to no distractions. You can keep sermon notes and return to them easily as the years go by, and search for keywords across your sermons and Bible study to strengthen your heart when you need that comfort most.

Lamped was built by OHHF, a foundation dedicated to improving the lives of orphans, single mothers and vulnerable communities. The best part? Lamped is free forever.

If you would like to donate to OHHF to help continue the mission and keep the light of this app on, we would be overjoyed. Visit ohh.foundation.
